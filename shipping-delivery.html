Shipping & Delivery Policy

This policy applies to physical products. Digital products do not require shipping and follow the Digital Products Policy.

Effective and last updated October 6, 2026

Physical products

Dyndel Store currently focuses primarily on digital and creative products. Physical products, such as small creative merchandise, may be offered when they are available. Shipping options are shown only where a destination and configured method are supported.

Processing time

After a physical order is successfully paid and accepted, please allow up to 7 business days for processing before shipment. Processing can include preparing, checking, packing, and handing the order to the selected delivery service.

Processing time is separate from shipping or transit time. The delivery estimate shown at Checkout begins after the order has been shipped unless the product or method expressly states otherwise.

Shipping availability and estimates

International shipping is supported only where Checkout offers an eligible method for the selected destination. Availability may differ by product, destination, and current shipping configuration. Dyndel Store does not promise shipping to every country.

Delivery ranges displayed during Checkout are estimates associated with the selected shipping method. They are not guaranteed arrival dates. Carrier operations, destination conditions, public holidays, weather, customs, and other events outside reasonable control may affect transit.

Customs, duties, and destination charges

Customers are generally responsible for destination-country customs requirements, import duties, taxes, handling charges, or similar fees where applicable, subject to applicable law. These amounts are not included unless Checkout expressly identifies them as included.

Dyndel Store cannot control customs inspections, clearance times, local carrier handling, or decisions by destination authorities.

Address and delivery issues

Customers should provide a complete, accurate delivery address. If you notice an error before shipment, use the Contact section as soon as reasonably possible. Changes cannot be promised after dispatch.

If tracking or other delivery information is available for a product in the future, it will be provided through the applicable fulfillment process. Shipment tracking is not currently promised for every method.

Third-party fulfillment

Some future physical products may be manufactured or fulfilled through a third-party service. Where relevant, product-specific information may identify additional production, delivery, or support conditions. No particular provider or physical fulfillment arrangement is promised by this policy.

Cancellations, problems, and rights

Physical orders may generally be cancelled before shipment. Once shipped, they cannot normally be cancelled. Damaged or incorrect items are addressed in the Returns & Refunds Policy.

Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law.
