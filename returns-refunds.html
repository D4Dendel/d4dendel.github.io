Returns & Refunds Policy

This policy sets practical rules for physical product problems, cancellations, and the limited exceptions that apply to digital products.

Effective and last updated October 6, 2026

Store policies

TermsPrivacyShipping & DeliveryReturns & RefundsDigital Products

Physical products

Returns, replacements, or refunds for physical products are primarily available when an item arrives damaged or when the wrong item was sent. Dyndel Store does not establish a broad change-of-mind return promise.

Use the Contact section as soon as reasonably possible after discovering a problem. Include enough order information to identify the purchase and describe what happened. Photographs of the item, packaging, or shipping label may be requested where they reasonably help assess damage or an incorrect shipment.

Available resolution

After reviewing the circumstances, Dyndel Store may offer an appropriate resolution such as correcting the order, replacing an eligible item where reasonably available, or providing a full or partial refund. The suitable result can depend on the issue, available stock, fulfillment arrangement, and applicable consumer rights.

Items should not be returned without instructions, because the correct return location or process may depend on how that product was fulfilled. Reasonable return costs for a confirmed damaged or incorrect item will be addressed as part of the offered resolution where applicable.

Physical-order cancellation

A physical order may generally be cancelled before shipment. Contact Dyndel Store promptly with the relevant order information. Once an order has shipped, it cannot normally be cancelled, although remedies for damage, an incorrect item, or rights required by applicable law remain available.

Digital products

Digital products generally cannot be returned or refunded after successful delivery or access. Limited exceptions may apply to a duplicate charge, corrupted file, inaccessible file, or genuine fulfillment or delivery problem. Dyndel Store should first have a reasonable opportunity to correct a technical access or delivery issue where possible.

Simple change of mind, an accidental purchase after successful delivery, or failure to read the product information does not normally qualify for a refund. See the full Digital Products Policy.

How to ask for help

Use the existing Contact section. Do not send payment-card credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information. A request does not guarantee a particular resolution before the facts and applicable rights are reviewed.

Nothing in this policy limits return, refund, cancellation, or other rights that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law.
