Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information Dyndel Store uses to operate Checkout, prepare and fulfill orders, support customers, and protect the Store.

Effective and last updated October 6, 2026

Store policies

TermsPrivacyShipping & DeliveryReturns & RefundsDigital Products

Who handles Store information

Dyndel Store is operated by independent creator Dyndel Pino. This policy applies to information handled through the Store and its existing Contact section.

Information involved

Depending on how you use the Store, information may include:

  • full name, email address, and optional phone number;
  • a shipping address when a physical order requires delivery;
  • Cart contents, product quantities, order details, prices, shipping selections, and order status;
  • messages and information you provide when requesting support; and
  • standard technical or security information legitimately processed by the website or hosting environment, such as request timestamps, IP address, browser/device information, and error or security logs.

The Store does not currently provide customer accounts, newsletter enrollment, or advertising profiles.

Why information is used

Information may be used to provide Checkout, prepare and process orders, arrange delivery or digital fulfillment when available, respond to customer support, maintain accurate operational records, detect misuse or fraud, secure the Store, resolve disputes, and meet applicable accounting or legal obligations.

Payment information

A future Checkout may use an external payment provider. If introduced, that provider may collect and process payment information under its own terms and privacy practices. Dyndel Store does not intend to store full payment-card credentials directly. No payment provider is currently identified by this policy, and payment processing has not yet been added to the Store.

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be shared only as reasonably needed with services that support hosting, security, payment processing when introduced, delivery, future product fulfillment, customer support, or professional and legal obligations. Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to protect customers, the Store, or others, or where required by applicable law. Dyndel Store does not claim to maintain an advertising database or sell customer information for targeted advertising.

Retention and security

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for order records, fulfillment, customer support, security, accounting or legal obligations, and dispute handling. Different records may need different retention periods.

Reasonable safeguards are used in operating the Store, but no website, transmission, or storage method can promise perfect security. Data deletion requests may be limited where records must reasonably be retained for an active order, security, accounting, legal obligations, or disputes.

Your choices and contact

You may ask about personal information associated with your interaction or request a reasonable correction or deletion through the Contact section. Requests may require enough information to identify the relevant person, order, or message. Available rights and exceptions can depend on applicable law and the circumstances.

Nothing in this policy limits privacy or consumer rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
