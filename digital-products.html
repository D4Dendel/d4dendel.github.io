Digital delivery

Digital products do not require physical shipping. Product descriptions should identify the expected file, format, access method, or other material details. Digital delivery or access will occur only through the fulfillment method available after successful payment verification. Secure digital delivery has not yet been added to the current Store.

Returns and refunds

Because a digital product cannot normally be returned after successful delivery or access, digital purchases are generally not returnable or refundable at that point.

Exceptions may include:

  • a duplicate charge for the same intended purchase;
  • a corrupted file;
  • a file that cannot be accessed through the provided method; or
  • another genuine fulfillment or delivery problem.

Where possible, give Dyndel Store a reasonable opportunity to correct a technical delivery or access problem first. Simple change of mind, an accidental purchase after successful delivery, or failure to read the product information does not normally qualify for a refund.

Requesting technical help

Use the Contact section with enough order information to identify the purchase and a clear description of the problem. Helpful non-sensitive details can include the device, application, file format, or error message involved. Do not send passwords or payment-card credentials.

Compatibility and access

Customers should review stated formats and requirements before purchasing. Dyndel Store will reasonably describe known requirements but cannot guarantee compatibility with every device, application, operating system, or future software version. Customers are responsible for retaining their lawfully delivered files unless the product expressly promises continuing hosted access.

Permitted use and intellectual property

Artwork and digital files remain protected by applicable intellectual-property rights. Purchasing a digital product does not transfer copyright or grant unrestricted resale, redistribution, commercial exploitation, or representation of the work as your own.

A product may include a specific license granting additional personal or commercial rights. Where provided, that product-specific license controls the permitted use of that product. No broader license should be assumed.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits digital-product remedies or other rights that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law.
